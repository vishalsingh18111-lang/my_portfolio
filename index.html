About Me

“Design is not just what it looks like. Design is how it works.”

I'm Vishal Singh, a Frontend Developer and UI/UX Designer from Varanasi. I hold a BCA (2022–2025) and completed the 402-hour Digital Content Creation program at Arena Animation (2024–2026), where I trained in UI design, web development and graphic design.

My real interest is where design meets code. I like starting with a wireframe in Figma, shaping it into a polished UI, and then building it myself as a responsive website. My background in Photoshop and Illustrator helps me care about typography, color and layout in every screen I make. I'm currently learning Angular JS to grow into framework-based development.
